# Super Mario 64 - Dream Machine
Inspired by Oasis and DeepMind's "dream" machines.

Q: What is a dream machine?
A: In simple words, it's just a software capable of predicting the next frame of the game feeding it with previous frames and pre-trained with millions of data (screenshots and videos of the game), all this done in real time, simulating a zero-engine game
Q: Why didn't you finish the project?
A: I'm too poor to afford a gpu.
Jokes apart, this is a little project I had in mind since a while, of course I can't continue the project due to the integrated GPU of my laptop, and CPU training is way too slow, using Google Colab was quite faster, going from 1 epoch in 7 minutes to 20 epochs in 7 minutes, not that fast, not that slow.
But this also means the prediction itself will be stupidly slow, destroying the whole purpose.
The current scripts are quite just a copy-paste with little edits, the model itself has issues with predictions, *probably* due to the low epochs amount and uncorrect CNN use.
I hope to continue and finish this project in the future, if I do, well, future myself, include this, as a keepsake.
